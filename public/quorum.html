Today · Manager view

Team availability

Availability is computed from sealed task hours against each person's daily capacity — no one sets their own status. Open a card to review the day and assign work.

Employee view

Your day & the vote

You see the tasks assigned to you and your resulting load — you can't edit availability. What you can do is vote on proposed tasks and their hour costs before they're sealed to the ledger.

Consensus queue

New tasks need a majority of the team to agree on the hour cost before they can be assigned to anyone.

Immutable record

The task ledger

Every agreed task is a sealed block: its hour cost, who voted for it, and a hash chaining it to the block before. Once sealed, the cost can't be quietly changed.

Propose a task

Opens a vote in the consensus queue. It's assignable only after quorum seals it.
